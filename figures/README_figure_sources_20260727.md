# DogMAG final figure source notes (20260727)

These SVG figures were generated from the July 23 DogMAG analysis tables and local release audit.
Visible figure titles and subtitles are intentionally omitted from the SVGs; titles and explanatory text
are carried in the manuscript figure legends.
Figure 2 reports assembly-class counts and read-manifest coverage for the final 41 BASALT input
assemblies. The full final 41-assembly span/N50/GC metric table was not present in this local copy
and should be copied or regenerated before adding assembly-metric distribution panels.
Figure 5 is the alluvial GTDB-Tk taxonomic composition view. Evolutionary placement visualisations are
deferred to a subsequent biology-focused analysis. The Figure 5 alluvial adapts the rank-collapsing logic
from `scripts/taxonomy_sankey.R` to the final DogMAG GTDB-Tk TSV without requiring R plotting dependencies.
Figure 6 reports the balanced RefSeq-versus-DogMAG read-recruitment validation and uses
manuscript-safe mixed-kennel source-cohort labels.
Figure 7 summarises the viral/prophage candidate counts and final CheckV-filtered resource layers.
